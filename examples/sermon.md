How to Love God

We love him, because he first loved us.
1 John 4:19 (KJV)
"Father, You loved us before we even knew what love was. Now, we can love You the way You want to be loved. Teach us how to love You, Lord!"

* If ye love me, keep my commandments.
John 14:15 (KJV)
Jesus, having the same requirements as His Father, God, did not leave us guessing about how He wants to be loved. He told us to keep His commandments.
Love for Jesus is not proven merely by how strongly we feel about Him, but by how faithfully we obey Him.

* He that hath my commandments, and keepeth them, he it is that loveth me: and he that loveth me shall be loved of my Father, and I will love him, and will manifest myself to him.
John 14:21 (KJV)
Jesus identifies those who love Him as those who possess and obey His commandments.
manifest: to exhibit to view, make visible, make known
Jesus promises to make Himself known to those who demonstrate their love through obedience.

* Jesus answered and said unto him, If a man love me, he will keep my words: and my Father will love him, and we will come unto him, and make our abode with him.
John 14:23 (KJV)
abode: to remain; abide; an abiding dwelling-place (i.e. not transitory).
Loving Jesus through obedience opens our lives to much deeper fellowship with Him and His Father.
